## University of Leeds — BSc Banking & Finance

*Where it all started: building a foundation in finance, economics, and quantitative research.*

### Dissertation: Private Equity's Impact on US Pharmaceutical Innovation

My undergraduate dissertation investigated how private equity investment influences innovation in the US pharmaceutical sector, using firm-level data spanning 1985–2023. I employed t-tests, OLS regressions, and random effects panel regressions to measure PE's impact on patent output, innovation value, R&D intensity, and financial health. The key finding: PE investment boosts innovation output and R&D intensity without the negative leverage effects that critics warn about.

> **Skills developed**: Econometrics, panel data analysis, academic research methodology, financial analysis  
> **View**: [Dissertation in US Pharma Private Equity Impact](./Portfolio%20of%20Work/Reports%20%26%20Investment%20Cases/Dissertation%20in%20US%20Pharam%20Private%20Equity%20Impact.pdf)

### Treasury Markets — Standard Chartered Bank, Dubai

During my time at Leeds I interned at Standard Chartered Bank in Dubai, working in Treasury Markets. This gave me firsthand exposure to institutional banking, FX, and capital markets operations — experience that later shaped my understanding of cross-border payment infrastructure and fintech opportunities in the MENA region.

> **Skills developed**: Treasury operations, FX markets, institutional banking, MENA market knowledge
